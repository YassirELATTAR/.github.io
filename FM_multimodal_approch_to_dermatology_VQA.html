Overview

This project focuses on developing a multimodal Visual Question Answering (VQA) system specifically for dermatological applications. By integrating both visual embeddings extracted from medical images and textual representations obtained from clinical descriptions, we aim to enhance AI-driven dermatology diagnosis.

The challenge in dermatology VQA is ensuring that the AI model correctly interprets complex medical imagery while effectively correlating textual descriptions to provide accurate answers. By leveraging advanced fusion mechanisms, we investigate how different architectures influence model performance and interpretability.

This project is based on our research presented in the paper A Multimodal Approach to Dermatology VQA, where we detail our methodology, dataset augmentation, and findings.
