Happy Birthday, Sarah
From someone who's thinking of you today 🌙

✕

Dear Sarah,

Today, the world is a little more beautiful because you're in it. I know I can't be there with you — no cake to share, no candles to blow together — but I wanted to make sure you knew how much today means.

You're someone who finds wonder in books and beauty in words. Someone who turns flour and butter into art, who sees the poetry in a cold morning and the magic in the first snowfall. You carry warmth with you, always.

On your birthday, I hope you feel it all come back to you — every kindness you've given, every bit of joy you've created, every beautiful thing you've noticed that others walked past.

You deserve a day as wonderful as you are. And I promise — next time, I'll be there to eat the cake in person. 🎂

With so much love,
always yours 💌
